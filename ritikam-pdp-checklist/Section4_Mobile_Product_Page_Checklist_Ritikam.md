# SECTION 4 | MOBILE SHOPIFY-STYLE PRODUCT PAGE CHECKLIST

**Worked example:** Ritikam Hammered Copper Water Bottle (with matching tumbler)
**Primary action:** Add to Cart

Open one product page or mobile mockup on your phone. Start with the first photo and follow the same path a buyer would take through checkout. Tick what you can verify and mark anything missing or unclear as **Fix**.

---

## 4.1 | Make Every Carousel Image Answer a Buying Question

- ☐ **The first image makes the product and its use obvious.** Show what it looks like in the situation the buyer came for.
- ☐ **The next images show details the buyer can't inspect in person.** Cover texture, finish, capacity, scale and packaging. Check that photos are easy to swipe and zoom on mobile.

**Ritikam example:** Start with the bottle being filled from the hammered copper jug at a kitchen counter, so the daily water ritual is obvious at a glance. Follow with:
- a close-up of the hand-hammered base and beaded band
- the brushed copper body and the cap's thread
- the bottle held in a hand for scale, beside the tumbler
- the inside of the bottle, so buyers can see the copper interior
- the box it arrives in
- the 10-second reel showing the bottle being filled and the cap closed

---

## 4.2 | Put the Product Name, USP, Price and Reviews Together

- ☐ **The title names the exact product.** The line below gives the buyer a specific reason to choose it.
- ☐ **Capacity and price are visible together.** Show the verified rating and review count nearby, with a link to the reviews.

**Ritikam example:** Use the exact product name, e.g. *Hammered Copper Water Bottle*, followed by "Fill it the night before, sip from copper all day." Show the actual capacity in ml, and the bottle's weight if buyers ask about it, right beside the price so the buyer knows exactly what they're paying for.

---

## 4.3 | Give the Buyer a Reason to Buy Now

- ☐ **The offer makes a first order easy to justify.** Consider a bottle-and-tumbler set, a printed first-use and care guide, or gift-ready packaging tied to why the buyer wants the product.
- ☐ **Any countdown reflects a real deadline.** Explain what the buyer gets by ordering before it, such as same-day dispatch for eligible orders.

**Ritikam example:** A bottle-and-tumbler set lets the buyer start the copper-water ritual for their whole morning in one order. If it's sold in a gift box, show the actual box, since many copper purchases are gifts for weddings, housewarmings and festivals. If there's a real dispatch cut-off, put it beside the buy button. Don't use a countdown that resets on every visit.

---

## 4.4 | Make It Easy to Choose, Check Delivery and Add to Cart

- ☐ **The selected capacity, set option, quantity and price stay clear.** Unavailable variants are visibly marked, and changes update the price where applicable.
- ☐ **The buyer can check delivery by PIN code before checkout.** Show serviceability and an estimated arrival date or range where available.
- ☐ **Add to Cart is easy to reach and preserves the selection.** If you use a sticky button, check that it opens any required selector and leaves controls and error messages visible.

**Ritikam example:** Choose a capacity (or "Bottle + Tumbler Set"), enter a PIN code and tap Add to Cart. Open the cart and check that it shows the exact capacity, set option and quantity you chose. If gift wrap is offered, check that it's also carried into the cart.

---

## 4.5 | Put Payment and Credibility Signals Near the Buy Button

- ☐ **The payment methods shown are actually available at checkout.** Include UPI, cards, wallets or cash on delivery only where supported.
- ☐ **Every credibility badge has a basis.** Only claim "Pure copper", "Handcrafted in India" or "Featured in" if you can show proof, such as a material test report, the maker's details or a link to the article. Surface a useful policy, such as what happens if the bottle arrives dented or the cap leaks, if the business offers it.

**Ritikam example:** Place the accepted payment methods directly below Add to Cart. Add a short link to the actual replacement policy for transit damage, so the buyer can check it before paying. Avoid unverified health claims about copper water. Describe the tradition, not medical benefits.

---

## 4.6 | Make Material, Usage and Care Details Easy to Find

- ☐ **The page states exactly what arrives.** Include:
  - capacity
  - dimensions and weight
  - material, and whether there's any inner coating or lacquer
  - what's in the box (bottle, tumbler, care card)
  - cap type
  - care instructions
- ☐ **Information that affects the purchase is easy to spot.** Keep key usage guidance visible and use accordions for longer details.

**Ritikam example:** Keep your actual usage note close to the offer, e.g. "For storing plain water. Not for hot, acidic or carbonated drinks" (if that's your guidance). Use accordions for the longer details:
- **What's Inside:** the exact contents of the box.
- **Care & Cleaning:** how to wash and polish it, and whether it's dishwasher safe.
- **Why Copper Changes Colour:** a short note that natural tarnish and patina are normal and easy to restore.

---

## 4.7 | Use Buyer Photos, Testimonials and FAQs to Resolve Doubts

- ☐ **Reviews answer questions buyers actually have.** Look for genuine feedback on whether the cap leaks, the weight, how the finish holds up, tarnish over time, the taste of the water, and gift-worthiness. Use customer photos with permission.
- ☐ **FAQs cover the remaining purchase questions.** Include how long to keep water in the bottle, cleaning, tarnish, delivery, returns and replacements where applicable.

**Ritikam example:** A buyer photo of the bottle on a desk or dining table, next to a glass, helps someone judge its size and how it looks at home. A review saying the cap doesn't leak in a bag, or that the bottle polished back to its shine after a month, helps a buyer decide whether it fits their daily routine.

---

## 4.8 | Test Cart, Checkout and Order Confirmation

- ☐ **The cart shows the correct item and lets the buyer edit it.** The full amount, including any delivery, COD or gift-wrap charges, is visible before payment.
- ☐ **The buyer knows what happens after payment.** The confirmation shows the order summary and how they'll receive delivery updates.

**Ritikam example:** Walk through a test checkout on your phone. Verify the selected capacity or set, the shipping charge and the final total. Check that the confirmation tells the buyer what they ordered and where to look for tracking, whether that's SMS, WhatsApp or email, whichever you actually use.
